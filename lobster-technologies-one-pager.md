# Lobster Technologies — One-Pager
### Sofia: The AI Sales Agent for African E-Commerce

---

## The Problem

African e-commerce SMEs are losing sales every day — not because they have bad products, but because they can't keep up with customer conversations.

- A customer DMs on WhatsApp at 10pm asking about a dress. No reply until morning. Sale lost.
- An Instagram comment on a product goes unanswered for hours. Buyer moves on.
- The business owner is the salesperson, the delivery coordinator, the accountant — and now also expected to be online 24/7.

Hiring staff to manage customer conversations is too expensive for most SMEs. Generic chatbot tools were built for Western markets — they don't understand the Kenyan buyer, don't speak Swahili, and don't integrate with M-Pesa.

**The result:** Massive revenue leakage from unanswered conversations and slow follow-ups.

---

## The Solution

**Sofia** is an AI sales and customer engagement agent built specifically for African e-commerce SMEs.

She lives on **WhatsApp and Instagram** — where African commerce already happens — and handles the entire customer conversation lifecycle:

- ✅ Responds to product inquiries instantly, 24/7
- ✅ Qualifies leads and guides buyers to a purchase decision
- ✅ Handles M-Pesa and payment-on-delivery workflows
- ✅ Follows up on abandoned conversations automatically
- ✅ Speaks English and Swahili naturally
- ✅ Hands off to a human agent when conversations get complex

Sofia isn't a chatbot. She's a trained sales agent who knows your products, understands your customers, and never sleeps.

---

## The Market

**Primary Market:** Kenyan e-commerce SMEs — fashion, beauty, electronics, and general retail sellers operating on WhatsApp and Instagram.

- Kenya has **~7.4 million SMEs**, the majority of which use WhatsApp as a primary sales channel
- Mobile commerce in Sub-Saharan Africa is projected to reach **$180B by 2025**
- M-Pesa processes over **$300B annually** — the infrastructure for digital commerce already exists
- No AI-native sales agent product exists today built specifically for this market

**Beachhead:** Kenya. Expansion path → East Africa (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda) → West Africa (Nigeria, Ghana).
